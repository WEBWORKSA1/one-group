# Competitive research: 30 sites in the group and community niche

We reviewed 30 sites: 26 by fetching their pages and 4 through search results. Kickstarter's page returned only metadata, so it contributed little.

## Sites reviewed

| # | Site | What we took from it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | meetup.com | "Near you" discovery, interest categories, city pages, 3-step how-it-works, separate "Start a group" path |
| 2 | eventbrite.com | Search with filters (today, this weekend), city cards, separate attendee and organizer journeys |
| 3 | disboard.org | Tag-based server directory, reviews, "bump" boosts for listings. Also a warning: weak moderation hurts trust |
| 4 | tgstat.com | Country, language and category catalog, ratings, analytics |
| 5 | skool.com | Category chips, cards showing members and price, affiliate program |
| 6 | reddit.com (searched) | Community rules, flair, volunteer moderators |
| 7 | facebook.com/groups (searched) | Public, private and hidden groups, membership questions, rule acceptance |
| 8 | nextdoor.com (searched) | Address verification, free verified business pages |
| 9 | circle.so | All-in-one platform pitch, security badges, "Start free trial" |
| 10 | mightynetworks.com | **Revenue calculator**, 14-day trial with no card, stats and case studies |
| 11 | heylo.com | Registration link that also collects waiver and payment, brand-sponsorship matching with revenue share |
| 12 | spond.com | "Who are you?" role split, free for teams, fundraising |
| 13 | teamsnap.com | Registration, payments, sponsorships, demo request |
| 14 | groups.io | Clear free and premium pricing, free migration help, logo wall |
| 15 | groupme.com | Download or open in browser, app-first CTA |
| 16 | discord.com | Safety Center, parental controls, earns from Nitro and boosts |
| 17 | signupgenius.com | "Free, no credit card", FAQ that answers objections, case studies |
| 18 | doodle.com | Group polls, booking pages, security badges |
| 19 | splitwise.com | Expense splitting, freemium Pro tier |
| 20 | gofundme.com | Create, Share, Receive flow; giving guarantee; trust hub |
| 21 | patreon.com | Monthly tiers with perks |
| 22 | buymeacoffee.com | **Amount presets plus message field**, recent-supporters feed |
| 23 | gleam.io | Action-based contest entries, referral bonuses, fraud checks |
| 24 | kickstarter.com | Only metadata returned: goal meter and backer pattern |
| 25 | bark.com | **Multi-step quote flow that asks for contact details last**, pros pay per lead |
| 26 | teambuilding.com | Quiz form (type, size, city, budget, goal), "free 15-min call" |
| 27 | ehealthinsurance.com (small group) | Insurance form fields (employees 2–49 / 50+), licensed-agent neutrality statement |
| 28 | eventective.com | Venue and vendor marketplace, state SEO pages, vendor lead plans |
| 29 | peerspace.com | What / Where / When search, "List your space" |
| 30 | groupon.com | Deal categories, urgency badges, refund promise |

Everyone deserves a group

Loneliness is widespread, yet thousands of great groups struggle to be found. One.Group connects the two.

Our mission

One.Group is an independent, platform-neutral hub. Whether a group lives on WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, Facebook, Slack or meets in a park every Saturday, we help people discover it — and we give organizers free tools, guides and better prices so they can keep going.

What we do

  • Directory — a human-reviewed index of groups by interest, city, language and platform.
  • Organizer toolkit — free calculators, planners and generators.
  • Playbook — practical, in-depth guides and videos.
  • Group quotes — one request, multiple offers for travel, venues, insurance, team building and bulk buying.
  • Contests & grants — prizes that celebrate and fund great groups.

How we make money

We are transparent: One.Group earns from display advertising (such as Google AdSense), sponsorships, featured listings, referral fees from quote partners and voluntary supporter contributions. Membership and basic listings stay free.

Our values

Neutral

We don’t lock groups into a platform.

Safe

Human review, reporting and clear standards.

Honest

Clear disclosures on ads and referrals.

Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. Paying does not increase your chances.
  2. Entrants must be 18+ or have verifiable parent/guardian consent. Void where prohibited by law.
  3. Entry periods, prize values and number of winners are announced on this page for each round.
  4. Winners are selected by a judging panel on the published criteria; community votes and referrals are advisory only.
  5. Winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days or an alternate winner may be selected.
  6. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Prizes are non-transferable unless we agree otherwise.
  7. By entering, you grant One.Group a non-exclusive licence to display your entry for contest promotion.
  8. We may disqualify fraudulent, duplicate, automated or offensive entries.

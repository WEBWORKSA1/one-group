Frequently asked questions

Members

Is One.Group free?

Yes. Searching, joining and listing a group is free.

How do I join a group?

Click “Request to join” on a listing. We pass your request to the organizer, or you follow the group’s own join link.

Are the groups safe?

Every listing is reviewed before publishing, and verified groups carry a badge. Always use judgement when meeting people — see our safety tips.

Organizers

How does a group get verified?

We confirm you control the group (e.g. by a pinned code or admin screenshot) and that it follows our standards.

What does a Featured listing include?

30 days of top-of-category placement, homepage rotation and a newsletter mention.

Can you help move my group from another platform?

Yes — ask via the contact form.

Quotes, contests & support

How do group quotes work?

You submit one request; up to five relevant vetted partners reply with offers. No obligation. We may receive a referral fee.

Do I need to pay to enter contests?

No. Entry is always free and payment never improves your chances.

Are donations tax-deductible?

One.Group is not a registered charity, so contributions are generally not tax-deductible.

Business

How can I advertise or partner?

See Advertise or contact the owner via the banner at the top of every page.

Is One.Group related to companies called “ONE Group”?

No. See our trademark disclosure.

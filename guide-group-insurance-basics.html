Group insurance explained

Employee health, group travel, event and club cover — in plain English.

By One.Group Editorial · 9 min read

This guide is general information, not insurance advice. Rules differ by country and region. Speak to a licensed professional before buying cover.

What is group insurance?

Group insurance covers multiple people under one policy, held by an employer, club, association or organizer. Because risk is pooled, it can be simpler and sometimes cheaper per person than individual policies.

Main types

TypeWho buys itCovers
Group healthEmployers (often from 2 employees)Medical care for employees, sometimes dependants
Group dental / visionEmployers, associationsCheck-ups, treatment, glasses
Group life & disabilityEmployersPayouts on death or inability to work
Group travelTrip organizers, schools, teamsCancellation, medical abroad, delays, baggage
Event liabilityEvent hostsInjury or damage claims at your event
Club / association liabilityClubs, sports teams, nonprofitsClaims arising from club activities

What affects the price?

  • Number of people and their age profile
  • Location and local regulation
  • Level of cover, deductibles and co-pays
  • Activity risk (hiking vs book club)
  • Claims history

Questions to ask any provider

  1. What exactly is excluded?
  2. Are pre-existing conditions covered?
  3. What is the claims process and typical payout time?
  4. Can members add dependants or extras?
  5. What happens if someone leaves the group?
  6. Is the adviser paid the same regardless of which plan I choose?

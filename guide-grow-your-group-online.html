How to grow a WhatsApp, Telegram or Discord group

Growth tactics that don’t turn your group into a spam channel.

By One.Group Editorial · 8 min read

1. Make the value obvious in the name and description

“Toronto Weekend Runners — Sat 7am social 5K” beats “Run Club 🏃”. Include who, what and when.

2. Onboard in under 60 seconds

  • Pinned welcome message with rules and the next event
  • An intro template (“Name · City · What brought you here”)
  • On Discord, a single #start-here channel and role selection

3. Build engagement loops

Weekly prompts, member spotlights, polls, challenges and wins threads create reasons to come back. Rotate hosts so it doesn’t depend on you.

4. Moderate early and visibly

Remove spam fast and explain why. Members stay where they feel safe. Recruit two moderators by 100 members.

5. Promote in the right places

  1. List on directories like One.Group
  2. Cross-promote with complementary groups
  3. Share public highlights on social media
  4. Add a join link to your email signature and bio
  5. Enter Group of the Month for exposure

Platform-specific tips

PlatformGrowth tip
WhatsAppUse Communities to split announcements from chat; cap chat groups to stay manageable.
TelegramPair a broadcast channel with a discussion group; use topics for large groups.
DiscordUse events, stage channels and roles; keep channel count low early on.

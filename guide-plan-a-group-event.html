How to plan a group event

Venue, budget, timeline and a printable checklist.

By One.Group Editorial · 8 min read

1. Define success in one line

“40 members meet, 10 new people join, we break even.” A clear goal decides every trade-off that follows.

2. Budget backwards

Start with what attendees will pay (or what sponsors cover), subtract a 10% contingency, and allocate the rest. Our event budget planner gives you cost per guest instantly.

3. Choose the venue

Venue typeGood forAsk about
Restaurant private room10–40 people, dinnersMinimum spend, set menus
Coworking / loftTalks, workshopsAV, cleaning fee, access hours
Hotel function roomConferences, galasCatering packages, room blocks
Park / outdoorFitness, picnicsPermits, weather backup

Request several venues at once via event & venue quotes.

4. Timeline

  • 6–8 weeks: goal, budget, date, venue shortlist
  • 4 weeks: book venue, open RSVPs, invite speakers/sponsors
  • 2 weeks: confirm catering numbers, promote again
  • 3 days: reminders, run-sheet, volunteer roles
  • Day of: arrive early, welcome table, name tags
  • Day after: thank-you message, photos, feedback poll

5. Fight no-shows

Free events often see many RSVPs not turn up. Reduce it with a small refundable deposit, a reminder 24 hours before, and a waitlist.

6. Make it memorable

Icebreakers, a clear agenda, one “wow” moment (a guest, a prize draw — see contests) and a reason to come back next time.

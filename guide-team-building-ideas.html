25 team building ideas for any budget

Virtual, hybrid and in person — sorted by cost.

By One.Group Editorial · 7 min read

The best team building doesn’t feel forced. Match the activity to a goal — bonding, collaboration, onboarding or celebration — and keep it optional where possible.

Free or almost free

  1. Two truths and a lie
  2. Show-and-tell of a hobby
  3. Walking one-on-ones
  4. Team playlist building
  5. Lunch-and-learn led by a colleague
  6. Online quiz with random teams
  7. Gratitude round at the end of a meeting

Under $25 per person

  1. Board-game café afternoon
  2. Virtual escape room
  3. Potluck with a theme
  4. Park picnic and frisbee
  5. Charity volunteering half-day

$25–100 per person

  1. Cooking class
  2. Bowling or karaoke
  3. Guided city scavenger hunt
  4. Improv workshop
  5. Pottery or painting session
  6. Hackathon with prizes

$100+ per person

  1. Sailing or kayaking day
  2. Team retreat / offsite
  3. Wine or food tour
  4. Adventure park
  5. Private chef dinner
  6. Sports day with coaches
  7. Multi-day group trip — see group travel guide

Make it work

  • Schedule inside working hours.
  • Offer accessible alternatives.
  • Collect feedback with a two-question poll.

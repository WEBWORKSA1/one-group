FAQ

Quick answers

Is One.Group free?

Yes. Searching, joining and listing a group is free. We are funded by advertising, sponsors, optional featured listings, partner referrals and supporter donations.

Do I need an account?

No. Request to join, list a group or ask for quotes using a simple form — no password required.

How do group quotes work?

You describe your trip, event or cover needs once. We share your request only with relevant, vetted partners, who reply with offers. You choose — or ignore them. There is never an obligation.

Is One.Group linked to other companies named “ONE Group”?

No. One.Group is an independent website. See our trademark disclosure.

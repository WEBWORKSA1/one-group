Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

One.Group (“we”, “us”) operates this website. You can reach us via our contact form.

2. What we collect

  • Information you submit in forms: name, email, phone, group details, quote requirements, job applications, contest entries and messages.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed, device and approximate location, collected by analytics and ad partners if you consent.
  • Local preferences such as theme and cookie choice, stored only in your browser.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to you and deliver the service you requested.
  • To share quote requests with up to 5 relevant partners only when you tick the consent box.
  • To send newsletters you opted into (unsubscribe any time).
  • To improve the site, prevent fraud and comply with law.

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers.

4. Processors

Form submissions are relayed by a third-party form-delivery service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Payments are handled by third-party processors (e.g. Stripe, PayPal); we never see card numbers.

5. Cookies & advertising

With your consent we use analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics) and advertising cookies (e.g. Google AdSense). Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised ads at Google’s Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. Choosing “Essential only” in our banner disables non-essential scripts on this site.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Canada PIPEDA/Québec Law 25), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, withdraw consent and object to processing. Submit requests via the contact form.

7. Retention

Lead and contact data: up to 24 months. Job applications: 12 months. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

8. Children

One.Group is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We will post updates here and change the “last updated” date.

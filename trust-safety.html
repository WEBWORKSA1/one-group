Trust & Safety

A directory is only as good as the trust people place in it.

Community standards

We do not list groups that promote hate, harassment, violence, illegal activity, adult sexual content, scams, pyramid schemes or the exploitation of anyone. Groups must describe themselves honestly, and paid groups must state prices clearly.

How listings are reviewed

  1. Automated checks for spam and banned terms.
  2. Human review of description, link and category.
  3. Optional verification of organizer control for a ✔ badge.
  4. Ongoing re-checks and removal of inactive or reported groups.

Safety tips for meeting a new group

  • Meet in public places for the first few events.
  • Tell a friend where you are going.
  • Never send money to individuals for “membership” outside a clear, official payment page.
  • Leave any group that makes you uncomfortable, and report it.

Safeguarding minors

One.Group is intended for users aged 18+. Groups for young people must be run by accountable adults or organisations and follow local safeguarding laws.

Report a group

Use the report form. Safety reports are prioritised and reviewed as quickly as possible.
